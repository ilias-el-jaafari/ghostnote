# Demo song attribution

All bundled GhostNote demo songs and their audio are **original and copyright-free**.
They were written and synthesized for the Kiroween 2026 Resurrection entry.
They are not based on, and do not sample, any existing commercial work.

| Song | Artist | Audio | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight Echo | The Night Owls | `midnight-echo.ogg` | Original chart + synthesized backing track |
| Aqua Parade | Glass Cabinet | `aqua-parade.ogg` | Original chart + synthesized backing track |
| Sky Capsule | The Hovering Lights | `sky-capsule.ogg` | Original chart + synthesized backing track |

The `.ogg` files are generated by `scripts/render-demo-audio.mjs` (procedural oscillators, drums, and pads passed through ffmpeg / libvorbis). You may regenerate them at any time.

You may use these demos with GhostNote. User-supplied UltraStar files and audio never leave the browser.
